== Disaster recovery demo ==
Objectives: RTO <= 60s, RPO <= 5 min
1) Primary running with 5 notes
2) BACKUP: <tmp>\backups\notes-20261124-001949.db
3) Two more notes written after the backup (these are at risk)
4) DISASTER: primary process killed and database destroyed
5) RESTORE: restored from notes-20261124-001949.db
6) FAILOVER: standby started on port 5102
   Notes recovered: 5 of 7 -> data lost: 2 notes
   Measured RTO: 1.18s (target 60s) -> MET
7) Corruption check: tampering with backup...
   Tampered backup: REJECTED, GOOD
OBSERVATION: Data written after the last backup is lost. More frequent backups or replication can reduce data loss.
